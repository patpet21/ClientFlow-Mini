## Descrizione breve (EN)

## Descrizione estesa (EN)
A light, practical workspace for busy solo operators and small teams who want to keep their customer follow-ups organized without learning a full CRM.

What you get:
- A clear dashboard of overdue and due-today follow-ups
- Client contact records, pipeline stages, notes and next steps
- Search and filtering to find the right conversation quickly
- Prewritten follow-up email drafts that open in your mail app
- Itemized quotes you can print or save as PDFs
- CSV client import/export and JSON backups
- Local browser storage and a responsive layout

**How it works:** Download and open the HTML file in a modern browser. Add your business information and your clients. That's it.

**Good to know:** This is an offline-first tool. It does not connect to your inbox, auto-send emails, sync between devices, send push notifications or process payments. It stores data in your browser; make regular backups. For reliable ongoing use, consider hosting the HTML page on your own HTTPS domain.

**You receive:** 1 HTML application, 1 getting-started guide, and example screenshots.
